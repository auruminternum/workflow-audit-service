Most automation prospects are not ready for a full build on the first call. They need a focused diagnosis of one workflow: where time is leaking, what should be automated first, what needs human review, and what a safe implementation would require.

The AI Automation Client Audit Kit helps freelancers and small agencies sell that first paid diagnostic.

Included:

- Discovery-call script for scoping one workflow.
- Workflow audit report template.
- Proposal and follow-up scripts.
- Delivery QA checklist.
- Sample audit and offline report builder.
- Pricing ladder for audits, implementation specs, and automation sprints.

Use it to package offers such as:

- $149-$349 workflow audits.
- $750-$1,500 implementation specs.
- $1,250-$5,000 automation sprints.
- White-label first-pass audits for agencies.

Boundaries:

- No client credentials required.

- No guaranteed clients.
- Commercial client use requires the agency license.
