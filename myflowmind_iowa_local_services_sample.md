# MyFlowMind Sample Lead Research Pack

Prepared as a public-source sample for an Iowa-focused automation agency.

Offer angle:
- Buyer: local service businesses in Iowa with quote/estimate/appointment workflows.
- Likely service fit: lead response engine, missed-lead follow-up, CRM capture, appointment/estimate routing, weekly reporting.
- Boundaries: public-source research only. No private scraping, no contact dumps, no automated sending.
