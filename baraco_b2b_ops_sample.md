# Baraco AI Sample Lead Research Pack

Prepared as a public-source sample for an AI automation agency focused on workflow automation, AI agents, email infrastructure, and data pipelines.

Offer angle:
- Buyer: B2B service companies with consultation, audit, quote, or assessment intake workflows.
- Likely service fit: lead intake routing, CRM updates, SLA reminders, sales/ops notifications, reporting pipelines, and AI-assisted qualification.
- Boundaries: public-source research only. No private scraping, no contact dumps, no automated sending.
