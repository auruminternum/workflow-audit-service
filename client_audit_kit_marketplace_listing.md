## Buyer FAQ

### Can I use this with clients?

Yes, with the agency license.

### Is this a lead list?

No. It is a delivery and sales kit for packaging workflow audits.

### Does it include automation builds?

No. It helps you scope and sell the audit/spec. Implementation is a separate service.

### Can I resell the raw templates?

No. You can use them in client delivery under the agency license, but not resell the raw kit as your own product.

### Do I need client credentials?

No. The audit can be done from notes, screenshots, exports, and process descriptions.
