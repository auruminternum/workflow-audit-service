# Insightful Agents Sample Lead Research Pack

Prepared as a public-source sample for an agency selling n8n workflows, HubSpot setup, RevOps cleanup, data pipelines, and workflow remediation.

Offer angle:
- Buyer: B2B SaaS, RevOps, and service companies with public signs of CRM workflows, HubSpot/Salesforce integrations, reporting pipelines, or multi-tool sales operations.
- Likely service fit: tool-bloat audit, HubSpot/n8n workflow cleanup, CRM hygiene, reporting automation, lead routing, and operational remediation.
- Boundaries: public-source research only. No private scraping, no contact dumps, no automated sending.
