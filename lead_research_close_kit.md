# Lead Research Close Kit

Public offer: https://auruminternum.github.io/workflow-audit-service/lead-research.html

Use these replies after a prospect responds to the lead research offer. Keep all outreach manual, honest, and scoped.

## Fast Qualification

Ask these before accepting payment:

1. What offer are you selling?
2. Who is the ideal buyer?
3. What geography or language should I target?
4. Which industries should I avoid?
5. Do you prefer CSV or Markdown?
6. Which package do you want: `$99`, `$199`, or `$349`?

## Reply: "How much does it cost?"

Short answer:

> The starter list is `$99` for 25 public-source prospects. The focused sprint is `$199` for 50 prospects with source links, fit notes, and personalized first-touch drafts. The pipeline pack is `$349` for 100 prospects segmented by offer angle. Work starts after scope and payment confirmation.

Longer answer:

> For a first test I recommend the `$199` focused sprint: 50 prospects, source links, fit notes, visible pain signals, and one manual-send first-touch draft per prospect. It is enough volume to see whether a niche has good outreach potential without buying a generic scraped list.

## Reply: "What do you need from us?"

> I need your offer, ideal buyer profile, target geography/language, disallowed industries, sources to prioritize or avoid, and preferred format. I do not need logins, private customer data, API keys, or inbox access.

## Reply: "Can you send the emails for us?"

> I do not offer automated sending or inbox management. I can write manual-send drafts and help structure the list, but your team should review and send messages through your own approved contact process.

## Reply: "Where do the leads come from?"

> Public business sources only: company websites, public directories, public GitHub or product pages, job posts, service pages, and other public context that indicates buyer fit. I do not use private data scraping, CAPTCHA bypass, or purchased personal contact dumps.

## Reply: "Can you guarantee replies or sales?"

> No. This is research and copy support, not a guarantee of replies, meetings, sales, or deliverability. The value is a cleaner prospect list and better first-touch angles for manual review.

## Simple Acceptance Criteria

The delivery is complete when:

- The agreed number of prospect rows is included.
- Each row has a public source URL.
- Each row has a buyer-fit signal.
- Each row has a suggested angle or draft message.
- The output follows the agreed format.
- No disallowed sources, industries, or outreach methods are used.
